Who we are. Kalzhan Studio is a photo and video studio run by photographer and producer Kalzhan Tursynbayev in Astana, Kazakhstan. Contact: WhatsApp / phone +7 702 183 38 36, Instagram @kalzhanstudio.

What this policy covers. Our business accounts on WhatsApp, Instagram Direct and Telegram are answered by an AI assistant ("Aigerim") that answers questions about our services and prices and collects booking requests. This policy explains what data the assistant processes and how.

Data we process

  • Your messaging identifier and public profile name (WhatsApp phone number, Instagram-scoped user ID and username/name, or Telegram user ID).
  • The content of messages you send us (text; photos you send may be viewed by the assistant to answer you).
  • Booking details you choose to give us: name, requested service, date, budget, contact details, location and wishes.

Why we process it

  • To answer your questions and reply to your messages.
  • To record your booking request and pass it to Kalzhan, who contacts you to confirm the details.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send you marketing messages you did not ask for.

Who processes it

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram Messaging API) and Telegram deliver the messages.
  • An AI language model provider (currently Google Gemini API) generates the assistant's replies from the conversation text.
  • Our own server (located in Astana, Kazakhstan) stores the conversation history and the booking log; booking notifications are sent to Kalzhan's private Telegram.

How long we keep it

Conversation history is kept for up to 12 months to keep context for your booking; booking records are kept as long as needed for the service and accounting, then deleted. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can always ask to talk to a person instead of the assistant — just write "Калжан" or "operator", or call +7 702 183 38 36.

TikTok publishing tool · Публикация в TikTok

EN. Kalzhan Studio uses a private, internal tool ("Zharkyn Publisher") to post the studio's own photos and videos to the studio's own TikTok account. Only the studio owner uses it. When the owner connects TikTok via Login Kit, the tool receives and stores on the studio server: the TikTok account's open_id, username and display name, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens. It uses them only to upload and publish content the owner has approved, and to check the publishing status. Photos for a TikTok post are temporarily hosted on this website so TikTok can download them, and are deleted after the post is processed. We do not collect data about other TikTok users, do not sell or share this data, and do not use it for advertising. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; on revocation or request we delete the stored tokens.

RU. Kalzhan Studio использует внутренний инструмент «Жарқын» для публикации собственных фото и видео студии в TikTok-аккаунт студии. Им пользуется только владелец студии. При подключении TikTok инструмент хранит на сервере студии open_id, имя пользователя и отображаемое имя аккаунта, а также токены доступа и обновления — только для публикации одобренного владельцем контента и проверки статуса публикации. Фото для поста временно размещаются на этом сайте, чтобы TikTok мог их скачать, и удаляются после обработки. Данные других пользователей TikTok не собираются, не продаются и не передаются. Доступ можно отозвать в TikTok → Настройки → Безопасность → Разрешения приложений; после этого токены удаляются.
