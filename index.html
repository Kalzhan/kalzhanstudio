Zharkyn Publisher

Zharkyn Publisher is the studio's internal tool for publishing the studio's own photos and videos to its own Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts. The studio owner sends a photo, album or video with a caption to a private Telegram bot, reviews the preview, chooses who can see the TikTok post and confirms. Only then the tool publishes it through the official TikTok Content Posting API. The tool is not offered to other users.
