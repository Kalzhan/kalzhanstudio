1. The service

Zharkyn Publisher ("the tool") is an internal tool of Kalzhan Studio, Astana, Kazakhstan. It publishes the studio's own photos and videos to the studio's own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) after the studio owner reviews and confirms each post. It is not offered to the public.

2. Who may use it

Only the owner of Kalzhan Studio and staff authorised by the owner. Access is limited to one private Telegram chat.

3. Content

Users publish only content that the studio owns or has the right to publish, with the consent of the people shown. Content must follow the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, and the rules of the other platforms. By publishing to TikTok the user agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

4. TikTok account

The TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.

5. No warranty

The tool is provided as is. Publishing depends on the platforms' APIs, which may change or be unavailable.
